Travel details

✈️ By Air

The nearest airport is Gaggal Airport, Dharamshala, about 60–70 km from Palampur.

From the airport, pre-booked cabs can take you to the venue in roughly 1.5–2 hours.

🚂 By Train

Nearest major railway stations are Pathankot and Chandigarh, from where taxis and private transfers are available.

From Pathankot, the journey to Palampur is scenic and usually takes 3–4 hours by road.

🚗 By Road

Palampur is well connected from Delhi, Chandigarh, and nearby hill cities by road.

We recommend leaving early to enjoy the beautiful Himachal roads and avoid traffic delays.
